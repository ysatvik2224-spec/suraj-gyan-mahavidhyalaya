Programmes & Subjects

Four programmes across teacher education, arts and commerce. Syllabi and examinations are prescribed by Bundelkhand University, Jhansi; the college teaches and conducts practical work for them.

Programmes Syllabus Teaching & evaluation Departments
🎓 Teacher Education

B.Ed. — Bachelor of Education

Two-year professional teacher-education programme with coursework in child development, pedagogy, subject methodology and supervised practice teaching.

  • Intake: 100 seats
  • Duration: As prescribed by the university — contact the office
  • Approval: NCTE · Affiliation: Bundelkhand University
Course details
🌾 Teacher Education

B.El.Ed. — Bachelor of Elementary Education

Elementary-level teacher training focused on child development, activity-based learning and classroom practice for the primary and elementary stages.

  • Intake: 50 seats
  • Duration: As prescribed by the university — contact the office
  • Affiliation: Bundelkhand University
Course details
🏕 Arts & Commerce

Undergraduate arts degree with the subject combinations offered by the university, as available at the college.

  • Intake: 420 seats

Teaching & Evaluation

How a session runs at SGM Konch, from classroom teaching to the university examination.

📚

Classroom teaching

Theory papers are taught lecture-wise according to the university syllabus, with blackboard work, discussion and revision before examinations.

🧪

Practical work

B.Ed. and B.El.Ed. trainees complete practice teaching.

📝

Internal assessment

Sessional and practical marks are recorded by the college and submitted to the university as per its scheme for the session.

📋

University examinations

Theory examinations are conducted by Bundelkhand University, Jhansi. The date sheet is displayed on the notice board and the college website.

📞

Academic help

Subject teachers and the office help with syllabus queries, examination forms, enrolment and document submission.

📅

Session & calendar

Admissions, teaching days, examinations and results follow the university calendar. Dates for the current session are announced on the student notices page.

Departments

Teaching at the college is organised into three departments, each handling its own subjects, practical work and internal assessment.

🎓

Teacher Education

B.Ed. and B.El.Ed. — pedagogy, child development, subject methodology, ICT and supervised practice teaching.

📜

Arts

B.A. — Hindi, English, History, Geography, Home Science, Political Science and Education.

💰

Commerce

B.Com — accountancy, business studies and economics, with tutorial support for university papers.
