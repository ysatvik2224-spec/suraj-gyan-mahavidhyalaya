Examination & Results

Theory examinations for all courses are conducted by Bundelkhand University, Jhansi. Date sheets are displayed on the college notice board and published above.

Before the examination

  • Check the date sheet on the notice board section and the university website
  • Carry your admit card and college identity card to every paper
  • Clear dues and fill the examination form at the office within the announced dates
  • Practical and sessional work is completed in the laboratory / practice-teaching hours

Results & marksheets

  • Results — affiliated colleges (Bundelkhand University)
  • Examination schemes & schedules
  • Examination formats & university notices

Marksheets are issued by the university; the college office helps with any correction or duplicate request.

Syllabus & Study Material

Subject-wise syllabus is the one prescribed by Bundelkhand University for the session in progress. Printed copies are available at the college office and in the library.

On campus

  • Printed syllabus copies for your course at the college office
  • Textbooks and reference books in the library and reading room
  • Practical manuals and laboratory records for science subjects
  • Competitive-exam and TET/B.Ed. entrance material in the library

Online

  • University syllabus & ordinances
  • Programmes and subjects offered at the college
  • Academics — syllabus & resources

The university updates its syllabus page each session — always confirm you are reading the current year's copy.

Scholarships & Student Support

State scholarship schemes, documents and the people to approach when you need help.

🎓

Scholarships

Scholarship and fee-reimbursement schemes announced by the state government are posted on the notice board each session.

  • Apply online on the UP Scholarship portal
  • Get your documents verified at the college office
  • Keep income, caste and domicile certificates ready
📄

Certificates & documents

Requests are handled at the college office with a short written application.

  • Bonafide / study certificate
  • Character certificate
  • Migration and duplicate marksheet assistance
  • Enrolment and registration help
🛡

Anti-ragging & grievance

Ragging is prohibited on campus. Complaints are handled by the college's anti-ragging committee and the Principal.

  • Give your complaint in writing at the office
  • Separate common rooms and safety measures for girls and boys
  • Talk to your class teacher for any academic problem first
