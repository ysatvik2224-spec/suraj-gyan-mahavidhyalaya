About the College

Our Story

Established 2010 · Panchanan Chauraha, Jalaun Road, Konch (Jalaun) U.P.

Suraj Gyan Mahavidyalaya was established in the year 2010 with a simple mission — to bring quality higher education and teacher training to the students of Konch and the wider Jalaun district. Located on Panchanan Chauraha, Jalaun Road, the college serves a largely rural and semi-urban student body with affordable, accessible programmes.

The college is affiliated to Bundelkhand University, Jhansi, and its teacher-education programmes are approved by the National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE). Spread over about 3 acres, the green campus houses classrooms, laboratories, a library and sports facilities.

  • Experienced and qualified teaching faculty
  • Library with textbooks, reference works and journals
  • Computer and science laboratories
  • Seminars, cultural programmes and sports through the year
