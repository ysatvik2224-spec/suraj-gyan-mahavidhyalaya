Admissions 2026-27

How to Apply

Admission is generally merit-based on 10+2 marks for undergraduate courses. B.Ed. and other professional courses may involve an entrance examination / counselling as per university norms. (EDIT: confirm current session rules with the office.)

  1. Enquire / fill the form

    Submit the enquiry form on this page, call the office, or visit the college in person.

  2. Collect the application form

    Collect the admission form from the college office (or as directed by the university).

  3. Document verification

    Bring original documents with two photocopies for verification on the notified date.

  4. Merit list / counselling

    Selection as per 10+2 merit, entrance test or counselling — whichever applies to your course.

  5. Fee payment & confirmation

    Pay the course fee at the office, collect the receipt and confirm your seat.

Documents Required

  • High School (10th) mark sheet & certificate
  • Intermediate (12th) mark sheet & certificate
  • Transfer Certificate (TC) from the previous institution
  • Character Certificate
  • Caste / income certificate (if applicable)
  • Passport-size photographs (6–8)
  • Aadhaar copy & any university-required forms

Eligibility at a Glance

  • B.A. / B.Com: 10+2, merit-based as per university rules
  • B.Ed. / B.El.Ed.: Graduation as per university norms; entrance test / counselling may apply

Always confirm the current session's eligibility, dates and fees with the college office before applying.

Admission FAQ

How do I get admission to this college?

Fill the enquiry form below or visit the office. UG admissions are generally based on 10+2 merit; B.Ed. and professional courses may require an entrance test or counselling as per Bundelkhand University norms.

What is the admission process for B.Ed.?

Submit the application with graduation marks, then appear for the university process (entrance/counselling) if applicable, and complete document verification and fee payment at the college office. Confirm the current session dates with the office.

Which documents are mandatory?

10th and 12th mark sheets, Transfer Certificate, Character Certificate, photographs and caste/income certificate where applicable. Carry originals plus two photocopies.

What are the course fees?

Fee structures change every session. Please contact the college office for the current fee details of your course.

Are hostel facilities available?

Contact the college office for current hostel and transport facility information.

Is there an entrance exam for B.Ed.?

B.Ed. admission may involve an entrance examination or counselling conducted by the university/authority as applicable for the session. Please verify with the office.
