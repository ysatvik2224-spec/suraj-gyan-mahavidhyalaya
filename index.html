About the College

Established in 2010, Suraj Gyan Mahavidyalaya at Panchanan Chauraha, Konch has been serving the students of Jalaun and nearby areas with quality higher education and teacher training.

  • Affiliated to Bundelkhand University, Jhansi
  • NCTE approved teacher-education programmes
  • Focus on discipline, values and academic excellence
  • Library, laboratories and ICT-enabled classrooms
